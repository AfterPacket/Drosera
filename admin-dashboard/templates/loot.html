What is here

A sample is stored once, by SHA-256, however many times it arrives — the sightings count is how often it was dropped and by how many addresses, which is the more interesting number. MD5 and SHA-1 are computed here for looking up in tools that still want them; the quarantine itself records only the SHA-256.

Re-scanning clears the stored verdict and lets intel ask VirusTotal again on its next poll. A first scan needs no button — that happens on its own. This is for the sample VirusTotal did not recognise the first time, which is the one worth asking about twice. Nothing is ever uploaded: lookups are by hash, and submitting a file would publish someone else's breach.
