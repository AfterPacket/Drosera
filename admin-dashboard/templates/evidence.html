Export bundles

A bundle is a ZIP holding the full record for one address:

report.htmlReadable summary: score breakdown, credentials, ranked commands and the transcript
events.jsonlEvery logged event for the address
fail2ban.logIts lines from the ban log — what the firewall was told to do, and when
commands.txt / .csvEvery command run, merged from the session history and the event log
identity.jsonThe tracked identity record
credentials.csvUsernames and passwords tried
sessions/Every recording, plus engagement.cast stitching them into one timeline
clips/Rendered video of the sessions
loot/Quarantined payloads with their metadata
MANIFEST-SHA256.txtDigest of every file, so the archive can be shown intact later

The bulk archive nests one of these per address under attackers/ and adds index.csv over all of them. Archives stream as they are built, so a large export starts downloading immediately rather than being assembled first.

Leave at 0 for everything. A honeypot accumulates thousands of addresses that knocked once and left; raising the floor keeps the bundle to the ones worth reading. For a single address, use the export button on its profile.
